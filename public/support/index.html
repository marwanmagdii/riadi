Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a field or court?
Choose a sport, pick a venue and time slot in the app, then confirm your booking.

How do I cancel a booking?
Open My Bookings in the app and tap Cancel. Refunds follow the venue's cancellation policy.

How do I list my sports facility?
Use our Register Your Field form.

How do I delete my account?
Follow the steps on our Account Deletion page.
